# Hurricane Relief System
Hurricanes and tropical storms result in an average of 10,000 deaths per year. Hurricanes form over warm ocean water as low-pressure systems and result in strong winds, tornadoes, floods, and heavy rain. These incidents cause many people to be stranded without essentials or medical care. Additionally, these storms can cause infrastructure damage, leaving many without homes or cars. The victims are left to start their lives over again, with little compensation, and hurricanes are only becoming more dangerous. With the increase in temperature due to climate change, there is warmer ocean water and moister air, resulting in stronger and more severe hurricanes.
The Hurricane Relief System is an application designed with the goal of assisting those affected by these storms. It will provide the users with preparation tips and the ability to request and receive disaster relief assistance during and after a storm. Users can access localized preparation guides, locate shelters, submit medical or supply relief requests, view affected locations, and receive information about nearby resources and road conditions. Volunteers and administrators can access and manage relief requests to help coordinate emergency assistance to bring users relief faster and more efficiently.
